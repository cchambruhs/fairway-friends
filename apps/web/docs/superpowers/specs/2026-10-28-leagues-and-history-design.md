# Fairway Friends: history, photos, and leagues

Status: revised proposal for user approval. User selected member-submitted cards with League Leader review, fixed-place or points-proportional payouts, and standard league/tournament-style points. Implementation is not started. Builder review complete for the initial proposal; review amendments before implementation.

## Intent
Extend the existing public clubhouse into a personal disc golf history dashboard and a place to participate in several independently managed leagues. Keep existing scorecard imports, duplicate protection, bags, profile claims, and public player statistics.

## Approaches
Initially the user selected leader-selected scorecards; they refined this to member-submitted scorecards requiring leader approval. Alternatives presented were scheduled events combining multiple cards, or automatically qualifying all rounds against date/course rules. Leader review fits casual rounds without allowing unrelated personal rounds to change league rankings. Combined multi-card events and automatic qualification are deferred. Support fixed-place payouts or a points-proportional pool, selected before Start.

Dad appears on 421 player rows before deduplication. There are three repeated course/layout/start-time keys; these are candidates for merging, not assumed identical rounds. There are 1,092 nonempty UDisc round-rating cells.

Fix the existing importer to parse UDisc's `YYYY-MM-DD HHmm-0500` dates explicitly, retaining local time plus offset. Remove only trailing unused hole columns using the Par row's actual hole count; preserve missing scores inside the played layout. Check supplied totals and relative scores. Preserve UDisc round ratings as UDisc ratings, never PDGA ratings. Retain original names, dates, and source metadata for audit.

Normalize whitespace and case for matching known names, but never merge different names solely on similarity. Use existing verified username mappings and Dad/@cchambruhs. Provide an owner import preview with exact duplicates, new rounds, identity mappings, and ambiguous collisions. Unresolved names remain separate provisional identities and can be mapped explicitly. Doubles team labels must not become individual rounds or be attributed to either member; display team results separately and exclude them from individual stats and league eligibility in this first version.

Reconcile CSV rounds against saved shared cards by local date/time, canonical course/layout mapping, pars, and matching player hole scores. Saved cards may contain only a subset of the CSV players; safely merge additional appearances only when the match is unambiguous. Conflicting scores or uncertain layouts/times require owner review. Re-imports are idempotent, including concurrent submissions. Recalculate personal statistics from canonical records without double-counting. Incomplete rounds remain visible and excluded from complete-round averages and wins. Display source coverage so partial histories are not described as lifetime statistics.

## Identity and photos
Reuse existing ChatGPT sign-in and one-account/one-profile claims. Claims remain self-asserted and must not imply PDGA or UDisc account verification. A signed-in player may edit only their claimed profile. The owner retains administrative recovery controls.

Allow image upload, replacement, and removal. Use durable R2 storage with randomized keys and server-checked size and image type (JPEG, PNG, WebP; maximum 5 MB). Avoid accepting active SVG content. Display a fallback initials avatar when unavailable. Photos are public alongside existing public profiles; explain this near upload controls.

Offer PDGA-photo import only for a linked PDGA number when a supported retrieval path has been verified. Current public API services documentation does not establish a player-photo field; do not advertise automatic sync as connected. A manual upload is the working fallback. A custom uploaded photo takes precedence; never overwrite it silently. Clearly identify photo source and last successful retrieval if PDGA import becomes available. No invented image URLs or identities.

## League setup and lifecycle
A signed-in player with a claimed profile may create multiple leagues and becomes their League Leader. Members may belong to multiple leagues. Each league stores name, description, start/end dates, explicit timezone (default America/Chicago), maximum counted cards per player, optional course/layout restrictions, optional member limit, buy-in amount and currency, prize description, optional added prize money, and payout notes. Money values use integer cents and are nonnegative. This version records amounts and leader-marked paid/unpaid status; it does not collect, hold, or transfer money. Describe prize pool as declared/recorded rather than guaranteed funds.

Lifecycle: draft -> active -> completed. The leader configures the draft, generates/revokes an unguessable invitation link, and shares it. A link opens a league preview and requires sign-in plus an explicit Join action; merely visiting must not join. Avoid email delivery integration in this release. Membership is unique per league/account/profile. Limit join/redeem abuse server-side. A league preview exposes rules, dates, buy-in, and prizes so members can review terms.

Only the leader presses Start. Starting validates settings and membership, freezes competitive settings, closes joining, and timestamps the start. Require at least two members. Draft members can leave; leader cannot leave without first cancelling an empty draft or transferring leadership (transfer can be deferred, in which case prevent leader departure). Active membership cannot change in this version. Leader may mark Complete at any point while active; show early completion clearly when before scheduled end. At end date show Awaiting finalization and stop accepting new selections. Completed standings are read-only. Freeze competitive settings at Start; prize notes and recorded payment status may remain editable with an update timestamp.

## Eligible cards and points
### Submission and approval
Add a League round? option to the existing shared-card import form. Default is No. Signed-in members selecting Yes choose one of their active leagues from a dropdown and submit. Anonymous users may still import personal cards; league submission requires sign-in, a claimed profile, and membership. The submitter's claimed player must be on the card. League choice must be checked on the server, not trusted from the dropdown. Permit submitting an already-imported card to a league without adding another database round. Bulk CSV history remains an owner import and never enters leagues automatically.

Persist the round in personal history independently from the league nomination. A nomination starts Pending and contributes no league points, counted rounds, or payouts. Show the submitter whether the card saved and whether league submission succeeded; failures must not imply both operations failed. Show Pending/Accepted/Rejected status. The leader has a review queue with source link, date, course/layout, participants, scores, eligibility results, submitter, and submission timestamp. Only that league's leader may Accept or Reject; record reviewer, timestamp, and optional reason. Leader submissions also go through explicit acceptance. Rejection affects only the league nomination, retaining the personal round.

Each league/canonical-round pair has one nomination record. Repeated or simultaneous submissions return its existing status. The leader can reopen a rejected nomination while active; submitters cannot evade rejection through a duplicate import. Accept is atomic and idempotent, enforcing eligibility and round limits at decision time. After the league end date block new nominations, but allow the leader to review existing timely nominations before finalization. Do not complete a league with unresolved Pending nominations; the leader must accept or reject them first. Pending nominations show only to the submitter and leader; public standings show accepted cards only.

Only completed individual rounds within league date bounds, on an allowed layout, and containing at least two league members with completed scores qualify. Proposed builder decision: a round must start at or after both the configured start date and the actual Start-button timestamp; earlier rounds do not become eligible by backdating a draft. Show this rule before Start. Nonmembers on the card do not affect placement. Each accepted card is one competition; cards on different layouts are not compared directly.

### Standard-style points
Replace the earlier proposed 10/7/5/3 schedule with this proposed default: one point for each eligible league player beaten or tied on an accepted card, including yourself. With four finishers scoring 50, 52, 52, 55, award 4, 3, 3, 1 points. Season points sum accepted-card points. Default to one open group with a factor of 1. Label these Fairway Friends league points; they do not become official PDGA points. The PDGA's official system uses players beaten or tied including self, within a division, multiplied by its division/tier factor and calculated weekly for leagues. The app's per-card casual format deliberately does not claim to reproduce sanctioned weekly/multi-card results. Sources: https://www.pdga.com/points and https://www.pdga.com/faq/leagues/points-and-rankings (checked October 28, 2026). Formal divisions and sanctioned-event factors are deferred; leader cannot change the scoring formula after Start.

When a player reaches the configured maximum card count, additional cards involving that player cannot be accepted; the server returns a clear explanation rather than silently omitting that player's score. Show per-player progress toward the limit. No handicap scoring in this release.

Season ties share rank. Display wins and rounds as secondary information, never as undisclosed tie-breakers. League Leader can remove a mistakenly selected card while active, with a visible activity record. Canonical round refreshes recalculate active standings and record an activity entry when scores change. Completed league results use a finalized scoring snapshot so future source edits cannot silently rewrite results. A card may count in multiple leagues independently, once per league. Database constraints and transactional operations protect joins, starts, card selections, and completion from concurrent duplicates or partial saves.

### Leader-defined payouts
Before Start, the leader chooses either Fixed by place or Proportional to points. Expose the payout rules on the invitation preview and league rules. Freeze mode, cash pool, fixed amounts, and prizes when the league starts, alongside competitive rules. Participation and payment tracking remain separate: payment status does not silently change point or payout eligibility.

Fixed by place: configure any number of places (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, etc.), each with an optional cash amount and optional named prize. Total configured cash cannot exceed the declared distributable pool. If season ranks tie, split the cash for their occupied places equally, including zero for unpaid occupied places. Example: a two-player tie at second splits second and third cash. Show unawarded money for places without finishers. Noncash prizes involved in ties require a recorded leader decision before finalization; no automatic duplication or silent allocation.

Proportional to points: each member with positive accepted points receives cash pool × their season points / total accepted league points. Example: 25 of 100 points earns $50 from a $200 pool. Use integer cents, floor exact shares, and distribute remaining cents by largest fractional remainder with stable membership-ID order as a disclosed penny tie-breaker. Display estimated payouts during play; finalize after resolving pending cards. If total points is zero, leave the pool unallocated and prevent payout finalization. Fixed cash in tie groups uses equivalent cent-exact allocation. Show the sum of all payouts and unallocated balance so it always reconciles to the configured pool.

On league completion save points, ranks, rules, pool, finalized payouts, and noncash prize decisions as one consistent snapshot. Leader may mark payouts paid afterward without recalculating standings. This is a payout ledger and calculation feature; transferring or collecting money is outside scope.

## Dashboard experience
Add Personal and Leagues tabs, keeping current public clubhouse navigation available. Signed-in users with claimed profiles land on their Personal tab. Anonymous users see public clubhouse statistics and a sign-in prompt for personal/league membership features.

Personal shows all available individual rounds across leagues and casual play: scores, to-par trends, UDisc round-rating trends where present, birdie/par/bogey distribution, aces, streaks, best rounds, course/layout records, rivals, and existing bag/brand settings. Keep course length separate from measured throw distance. Unavailable measurements stay absent or explicitly unavailable.

Leagues shows memberships, creation and invitation joining, league status, personal rank, total points, cards counted/limit, next/end date, prize/buy-in summary, estimated/final payouts, and the user's submissions. Opening a league shows its standings, accepted cards, points history, members, rules, and leader review queue when authorized. Use responsive tables and readable phone layouts, loading states, accessible form errors, and clear empty states. Public visitors can view non-sensitive league standings; invitation tokens, account identifiers, pending nominations, and individual payment statuses are never in public payloads.

## Non-goals
Automatic UDisc round discovery, PDGA authentication/account verification, guaranteed PDGA-photo synchronization without a verified source, payment processing, sanctioning, handicaps, doubles league scoring, and multi-card combined events are outside this release.
